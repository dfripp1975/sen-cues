Questions

Before you start

Will it spoil the ending?+

No. You set your chapter and our guides, questions and AI notes are written for chapters up to that point. Move your bookmark forward when you are ready for more.

Does every book have a chapter guide?+

93 classics have built-in guides for key chapters, marked “Chapter guides” on the shelf. For any other book or chapter, sign in and the AI guide writes notes for exactly where you are.

Do I have to read what everyone else is reading?+

No. There is no monthly pick and no reading list. Bring whatever you are reading, at whatever speed suits you.

Do I need an account?+

Not to explore books or use the 20 tools. A free account saves your library, your chapter and your reading goals.

What if my book is not on the shelf?+

Add it to your library with its title, author and number of chapters, and the reading tools work for it straight away.

Is it really free?+

Yes, to start. 50 classics are free to read in full, plus chapter 1 of every other book, all 20 tools and the book club features. Premium opens all 519 books you can read here.
