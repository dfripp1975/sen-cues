How will I know an event is slipping?

Event Rescue Mode compares your sales with the pace your target needs.

  1. A risk level from your own pace

    On track, watch, behind or at risk, read from your recorded sales.

  2. The tickets a day you need

    Tickets still needed, divided by the days left before the event.

  3. Recommendations for this event

    Drawn from its category, description, booking link, prices and channels.

  4. A plan you can track

    Assign tasks, set deadlines, then watch what happens to sales. See it on a demo event.

How is the forecast worked out?

From your own recorded sales, with the sums shown beside every forecast.

Questions organisers ask

Does EventFill sell tickets?

No. It works alongside the platform you already use. You keep selling through Eventbrite, Ticket Tailor, Humanitix or your own site, and use EventFill to plan, track and improve promotion.

Does it sync automatically with Eventbrite?

Not yet. Enter sales by hand or upload a CSV export from your booking platform. EventFill shows where each figure came from and when it was last updated. Official integrations are on the roadmap.

Can EventFill guarantee I’ll sell more tickets?

No tool can honestly promise that. EventFill helps you see problems earlier, focus on the channels that spark interest and act on a clear plan.

Is the AI optional?

Yes. The AI Content Studio is optional and has monthly limits on each plan, so costs stay predictable. Everything else works without it.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your billing settings. Paid features stay available until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Where is my data stored?

In a managed PostgreSQL database with row-level security, so each organisation can only reach its own records. See the Privacy Policy for details.
